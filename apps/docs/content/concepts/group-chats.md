A group chat is one conversation between you and two to six of your bots. Everyone sees the same transcript, and every message shows who sent it. Group chats are separate from each bot's own bot chat.

## Who answers

- A message without mentions wakes every bot in the group.
- Mention bots with `@`, or reply to one of their messages, to wake only those bots.
- A bot's message wakes only the bots it mentions.

A bot that has nothing to add can stay silent or react with an emoji.

## Limits

- **Turn limit:** after 8 bot turns in a row without a message from you, the group pauses until you write again.
- **Late joiners:** a bot added after the first message gets a summary of the conversation so far.
- **No background work:** in a group chat, bots can't start coding jobs or desktop jobs, and can't hand work to other bots.
- **Private memory only:** bots can save to their own memory, not to shared memory.
