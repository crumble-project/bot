title: Create your first bot
description: Give a bot a name, a model, and instructions, then start chatting.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

1. In the web app, click **+** next to **Bots** in the sidebar.
2. Pick an avatar and a color, and enter a name.
3. Choose a model and a reasoning level.
4. Write the bot's instructions: who it is, what it's responsible for, and how it should talk to you.
5. Turn on the [capabilities](/concepts#capabilities) you want. You can change them later.
6. Click **Save**.

The bot's chat opens. Send it a message.

## What to try next

- Ask the bot to remind you of something next week. Every bot can [schedule tasks](/concepts/schedules-and-integrations#scheduled-tasks).
- Tell it something about yourself and ask it to remember. This needs the **Memory** capability.
- Add a second bot and start a [group chat](/concepts/group-chats).
